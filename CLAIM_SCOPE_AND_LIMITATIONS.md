# Scope and limitations

The output is the exact rational probability of a contiguous substring event between two independent draws with independent position-specific letter laws. The hardness uses binary fixed and fair positions and a one-call counting reduction with arithmetic postprocessing. The fair-bit count counts joint outcomes. For general rational inputs, the scaled numerator over a specified common denominator is the integer #P function.

The lower bound permits position-dependent probabilities. The case of stationary i.i.d. word laws and the distinct uniform-text existential-wildcard problem are outside its scope. The relative approximation concerns the occurrence probability. The conditional sampler has expected polynomial running time. Both follow by applying Karp–Luby–Madras coverage to this setting.

The replay provides finite numerical checks. External peer review, formal verification and an exhaustive priority search remain pending.
