How the residue works

For fixed pattern bits, a text block on a covered edge cannot match. Its contribution to the nonmatch count is 16. An uncovered edge has one matching outcome, leaving 15. Multiply these contributions, then sum over the eight possible patterns. Modulo 15, a cover contributes 1 and every other pattern contributes 0.
